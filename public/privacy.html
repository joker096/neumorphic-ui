Mess&Anger — Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 10, 2026

The short version

Mess&Anger is an end-to-end encrypted peer-to-peer (P2P) messenger. You do not create an account: your identity is a cryptographic keypair generated on your device. We never see your message content, and we collect the minimum technical data needed to relay connections, serve in-app ads, and process premium purchases.

How the app works

  • Messages are end-to-end encrypted. Content is encrypted on your device with X25519 ECDH + Ed25519 signatures. The relay server only ever sees encrypted blobs — it cannot read your messages, contacts, or files.
  • No account, no email, no phone number. Your identity is a local recovery phrase / master seed stored only on your device. The server recognizes you solely by your Ed25519 public key (a pseudonymous identifier).

What we collect

  • Connection record. Each time your device connects to the relay: your device public key, IP address, user agent string, country, and connect/disconnect timestamps. Used to operate and secure the connection service.
  • In-app ad events. Mess&Anger shows first-party ads (created and hosted by us, with no third-party advertising SDK). We record impressions and clicks per device public key to measure performance. Ads are not personalized using message data, contact lists, or personal information.
  • Payment records (premium only). If you purchase a premium subscription, the payment is processed by a third-party payment gateway (paymento.io). We store the resulting order metadata: order ID, amount, currency, status, and your device public key — needed to grant and verify entitlements. Card details are handled exclusively by the payment gateway; we never see your full payment details.

What we do NOT collect

  • Message content, attachments, or media — encrypted end-to-end, readable only by the participants.
  • Email address, phone number, or real name.
  • Contact lists — stored only on your device.
  • Third-party analytics, tracking pixels, or advertising SDKs. The app contains no external analytics or tracking code.
  • Cookies and tracking pixels. Sessions are device-local.

What stays on your device

Your identity (recovery phrase / master seed), contacts, chat history, and media cache are stored locally in your browser / app storage. This data never leaves your device except when you send it (encrypted) to another participant, or when you use a payment feature.

Data retention and deletion

Connection logs and ad events are kept while we operate the service. Payment order records are kept as required to support subscriptions and for legitimate accounting.

You may request access to, or deletion of, any data stored against your device public key. Because your identity is a public key you hold locally, you can also permanently leave the service by generating a fresh identity on your device — your old key simply stops connecting.

Your rights

  • Access: request the data we hold for your device key.
  • Correction: no personal attributes to correct beyond connection metadata.
  • Deletion: request removal of connection logs, ad events, or payment records.
  • Opt-out of ads: ads are the free tier's funding source; a premium subscription removes ads.

Third parties

  • paymento.io — payment processor for premium subscriptions. Subject to their own privacy policy; they receive only what is required to process a payment.
  • Hosting — the relay server is hosted by our provider; connection metadata is stored there under our control.
  • No advertising networks, no analytics vendors, no data brokers.

Children

The service is not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.
