01 · Mesh Network

DHT-based node discovery & multi-hop relay

Reticulum-inspired mesh architecture. DHT node discovery, TTL-based routing tables, and multi-hop relay for anonymous, resilient communication even behind NATs and firewalls.

02 · Store-and-Forward

Message envelopes with TTL expiration, acknowledgment tracking, and forward secrecy — derived message keys are never stored after use. Works offline and in mesh scenarios.

Signal Protocol double ratchet with skipped message key decryption. Each message key is derived but never stored after use — even if your device is seized, past messages stay safe.

Anti-censorship

5 resistance levels. If one is unavailable — automatic switch. Traffic is indistinguishable from regular HTTPS for any DPI.

Decentralized node discovery with latency-based sorting. TTL-based routing tables with automatic cleanup.

Multi-hop Relay

Messages traverse multiple relay nodes. Each hop strips routing info — sender and recipient are hidden.

Store-and-Forward

FAQ

Frequently
asked questions

What happens if I lose my phone?
If you created a backup (.mess file) — restore your account on a new device. Without backup, data is lost forever.
Can the government block Mess&Anger?
Practically impossible. Traffic is obfuscated and indistinguishable from regular HTTPS. If WebRTC is blocked — WebSocket tunnel is used automatically.
Can someone read my messages?
Only you and the recipient. Messages are encrypted on your device and decrypted only on the recipient's device.
What is post-quantum protection?
Encryption resistant to quantum computers. ML-KEM-768 approved by NIST in 2024.
How do I add a contact?
Scan a QR code, search by username, or receive a nexus:// link. There is no central address book.
Is Stars a cryptocurrency?
No. Stars is a local reward system for activity. No blockchain, no market value.
